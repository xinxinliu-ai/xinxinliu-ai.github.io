Hi! I'm a first-year CS Ph.D. student at the University of Central Florida, advised by [Dr. Yuzhang Shang](https://42shawn.github.io/) and [Dr. Chen Chen](https://www.crcv.ucf.edu/chenchen/). Before that, I received my master's degree from Johns Hopkins University.

My research centers on **world model**, **embodied AI**, and **reinforcement learning**.

In research, I like to *ride the wave*: I'm willing to step out of my comfort zone for directions and methods I believe are right. Beyond that, I care most about work that brings *real-world value*.
